## English

ColorLand’s color-mixing game works offline and requires no account. Introduction screens are fetched online through Setgreet. If they cannot load, the game remains available. There are no advertisements or real-money purchases.

Progress, coins, stars, best mixes, tools, the daily-gift date, language and introduction-completion preference are stored on your device. Your operating system’s backup settings may include this local data.

Setgreet processes an SDK-generated anonymous installation identifier, app/device/language information and introduction interactions such as screen views, navigation and completion. These support flow delivery, localization and introduction analytics. ColorLand does not send names, email addresses, account profiles, saved game progress or custom gameplay events to Setgreet. App colors and font settings are also synchronized so the introduction matches the game. No cross-app advertising tracking is configured.

Resetting progress in Settings removes the saved game, coins, stars and tools while keeping language and introduction preferences. It does not delete Setgreet’s server-side introduction analytics. Removing the app removes local app data; operating system backups are managed separately. For privacy questions or requests about service-side data, email yunusoz86@gmail.com.
