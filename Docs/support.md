# ColorLand — Support / Destek

ColorLand: Mix & Paint / ColorLand: Renk Atölyesi

## English

For help, feedback or privacy questions, email [yunusoz86@gmail.com](mailto:yunusoz86@gmail.com). Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level or screen where you need help. Never send passwords or Apple account credentials.

The game has 40 levels and supports English and Turkish. No account, advertising or real-money purchases are required. Game progress is stored on your device. Resetting progress in Settings is permanent; removing the app can remove your saved game. Introduction screens need internet access; the game can be played offline.

[Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md)

Ayarlardan ilerlemeyi sıfırlamak kalıcıdır; uygulamayı silmek oyun kaydını kaldırabilir. Tanıtım ekranları internet gerektirir; oyun çevrimdışı oynanabilir.
